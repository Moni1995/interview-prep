Probability Interview Prep

An interactive study environment for data-science interview probability — built with plain HTML/JS.

📘 Interactive Study Guide Part I fundamentals + Part II advanced. ICAP-structured: explorers & simulators, predict-first prompts, self-checks, constructive exercises, optional AI tutor, and Mistake Log / snapshot export. ⏱️ Mock Exam — 40 Questions Timed, confidence-rated, answers hidden until you submit. Generates a detailed report you can copy for review.
The optional AI tutor asks for a DeepSeek API key; it is stored only in your own browser and sent only to api.deepseek.com. Progress and notes live in your browser's localStorage.
